# 🧠 EchoMind - Your Mind, Always Remembered

## 🚀 What is EchoMind?

EchoMind is a **private voice memory assistant** that lives entirely on your computer. It listens to what you say, turns your speech into text, and lets you ask questions about anything you've ever told it. Think of it as a second brain that remembers everything—without sending your voice or words to any cloud server.

**No internet? No problem.** EchoMind works completely offline. Your voice data stays on your device. Always. Period.

---

## 🎯 Why You Need EchoMind

Have you ever forgotten a brilliant idea before you could write it down? Or struggled to recall what someone said in a meeting last week? EchoMind solves that by:

- 🎙️ **Capturing speech** in real-time
- 📝 **Transcribing** it into searchable text
- 💬 **Answering questions** based on what you've said
- 🔒 **Keeping everything private** on your own computer

This is especially helpful for people with hearing difficulties, memory challenges, or anyone who thinks faster than they type.

## 🔍 How to Ask Questions

Once you've captured some speech, you can ask EchoMind anything about what you've said. For example:

- *"What did I say about my doctor's appointment?"*
- *"When did I mention my project deadline?"*
- *"What was the name of that restaurant I talked about?"*

EchoMind uses a special technology called **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** to find the right answers from your personal voice history. It's like having a search engine for your memories.

## 📚 Understanding the Technology

If you're curious, here's what powers EchoMind:

- **Speech-to-Text:** Uses OpenAI's Whisper model for highly accurate transcription
- **Local LLM:** Uses Llama.cpp to run language models directly on your computer
- **RAG:** Retrieval-Augmented Generation to answer questions based on your specific recordings

All of this happens locally, ensuring speed and privacy.
